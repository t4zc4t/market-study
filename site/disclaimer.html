What a score means and what it does not

Each check compares a figure taken from public filings with a threshold that was written down before any data was collected. A result of mildly or well outside the range only means that a figure is unusual compared with that threshold. There are often ordinary business reasons for that, such as a company selling mostly to its parent, a lending arm inside a group, a merger, a reclassification of shareholders or fast growth, and the company pages note these where they were found.

A score is not an allegation, a finding or an opinion that any company, promoter, director, auditor, employee or other person has done anything wrong, broken any law or misreported anything. It is not an audit, a forensic review, a credit rating or a measure of the quality or value of a business. Companies were picked at random and are listed alphabetically, and the site does not rank them.

Data and accuracy

All figures come from public filings made by the companies to the National Stock Exchange of India and from their published annual reports, downloaded on 8 October 2026 and covering filings up to 31 March 2026, and from SEBI's published orders. Some figures were read by hand from annual reports, and some company filings were found not to add up, which the method page explains. Mistakes are possible in how figures were read, worked out or shown. Everything is provided as is, without any warranty of accuracy, completeness or fitness for any purpose, and it is not updated as new filings come out. Always check the original filing, which every figure links to.

Corrections and requests for review

Anyone, including any company named here, can point out an error or ask for a figure to be reviewed by opening an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Each request is checked against the original filing. If a figure is wrong it is corrected and the change is recorded with its date in the corrections log. A company can also ask for its own explanation of a result to be noted on its page. While a disputed figure is being checked, its page can be taken down on request.
